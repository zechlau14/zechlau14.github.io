## Ingredients

* [Pie Crust](/recipes/Pie-Crust)
* 100g brown sugar
* 2 large eggs
* 100g chopped pecans
* 120g melted butter
* 2 tbsp milk (or water)
* 1 tbsp all purpose flour
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 50g pecan halves

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 160C.
2. Roll out pie dough and place into pie dish.
3. In a large bowl, beat brown sugar and eggs until creamy.
4. Add chopped pecans, melted butter, milk, flour and vanilla extract.
5. Pour filling into pie dough. Add pecan halves on top.
6. Bake for 50-55 minutes.
