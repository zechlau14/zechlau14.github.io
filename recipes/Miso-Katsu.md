## Ingredients

### For Katsu

* 2 pieces of chicken breast or pork loin
* salt and pepper
* 2 tbsp flour
* 1 egg
* 1/2 cup panko

### For Sauce

* 1/2 tbsp mirin
* 1 tbsp sake
* 1/4 cup dashi
* 1 tbsp sugar
* 2 tbsp miso

## Instructions

## For Katsu
1. Pound meat to about 1/2 inch thick.
2. Season meat.
3. Dredge meat in order of flour, egg, panko.
4. Fry (or bake)

### For Sauce
1. Heat mirin and sake to evaporate alcohol.
2. Lower heat and add sugar, dashi and miso.
3. Cook until thickened.
