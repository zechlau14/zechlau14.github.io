## Ingredients

* 3-4 Onions
* 1.5 tbsp Flour
* 1 cup rice
* 1 cup lentils
* 1/2 tsp minced garlic
* cumin
* black pepper

## Instructions
1. Cook lentils in pressure cooker.
2. Slice onions. Then toss with flour and salt.
3. Fry the onions to caramelise them. Set aside.
4. In a large pot, stir onion oil with dried rice for a minute.
5. Add lentils and seasonings: cumin, salt and black pepper.
6. Add water to the pot, then cook rice.
7. Serve lentil rice topped with fried onions.

Suggested sides: yogurt sauce and cucumber-tomato salad.
