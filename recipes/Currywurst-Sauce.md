## Ingredients (makes 1.5 cups)

* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1/2 onion
* clove of garlic
* 1.5 tbsp curry powder
* 1 cup ketchup
* 5 tbsp chicken broth / water
* 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
* 1 tbsp honey
* 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
* salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Cook onions until soft and translucent.
2. Add garlic and curry powder.
4. Add ketchup, chicken broth, vinegar, worcestershire sauce, honey. Simmer for 15 minutes.
