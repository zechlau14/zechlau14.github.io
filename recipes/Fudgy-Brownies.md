## Ingredients

* 180g unsalted butter, cubed
* 110g chocolate, coarsely chopped
* 1 cup sugar
* 3 large eggs
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 84g dutch-processed cocoa
* 120g flour
* 3/4 tsp salt
* 1 cup chocolate chips (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 175 C. Prepare an 9" square pan.

2. Melt butter with half the chocolate. Leave aside to cool.

3. In a mixing bowl, beat sugar and eggs.
4. Add melted butter and chocolate and vanilla extract.
5. Fold in flour and cocoa powder.
6. Add in remaining chocolate and chocolate chips.
7. Pour batter into baking pan and bake for 28-32 min, until the top is set.
8. Cool for 15 min before cutting.
