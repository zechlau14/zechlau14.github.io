## Ingredients
* 30-40g sourdough starter
* 200g water
* 15g milk powder
* 90g whole wheat flour
* 210g bread flour
* 6g salt
* 15g butter / olive oil

## Instructions
1. Mix sourdough starter with water and milk powder.
2. Add in flours and salt. Knead to combine.
3. Set aside for 20-30 minutes.
4. Knead in butter or oil.
5. Set aside for 30 minutes.
6. Stretch and Fold.
7. Place into bread tin and leave to rise (about 7 hours).
8. Bake at 210C for 30 minutes.
