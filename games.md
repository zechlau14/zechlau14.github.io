## Solo Games

### Comic Book Death
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/5337529" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/comic-book-death-legacy-heroes">Comic Book Death &amp; Legacy Heroes by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
Comic book characters are always dying, then coming back. On the big screen alone, we’ve seen Superman do so in Justice League and half the Marvel Cinematic Universe die and come back after the Blip. Some characters, like Jason Todd, are even defined by the manner of their death and return.

Comic Book Death is a solo game in which you play as the Hero who died and returned. The game guides you to journal about your experience in the Afterlife, and how you find your loved ones and the City that you protected when you return to life. This game is based on Anamnesis by Samantha Leigh, used under CC By 4.0, and requires a tarot deck to play.

### Poacher
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/5109336" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/poacher">Poacher by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
Play as a poacher who has come to the Sacred Forest of the Moon Goddess in order to fulfill the biggest order of their illicit career: hunt and skin seven of her silver mousedeer. Survive the enchanted realm and its wardens, riches beyond their wildest dreams await.

### Ninth Seed
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/5071371" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/ninth-seed">Ninth Seed by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
You are the coach of a professional basketball team that has squeaked into the post-season via the play-in tournament. With every win in the playoffs, your team grows in belief. Can you overcome the odds and coach your team to a championship?    

Ninth Seed is a solo TTRPG based on the Medusalaire system, by Brite Palette. To play this game, you require a standard deck of playing cards, and dice (1d6, 1d8).

### Echoes of a Masquerade
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/4773277" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/echoes-of-a-masquerade">Echoes of a Masquerade by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
Your fairy tale love story started at a masquerade. By some unknown means, you have returned to the night of the ball. Will you still live happily ever after? 

This game was inspired by Cinderella III: a Twist in Time. To play, you need two six-sided dice (2d6) and something to journal in.

### 5 Nights of Overtime
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/2968084" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/5-nights-of-overtime">5 Nights of Overtime by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
You are an ordinary office worker. During your overtime, you notice strange things occurring in the office. Over the week, your labor and the weirdness wears down your mind, body and soul. As the weirdness escalates into danger, will you survive the five nights of overtime? This game was inspired by 5 Nights at Freddy's.

### I Love You But We Must Fight
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/2621161" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/i-love-you-but-we-must-fight">I love you but we must fight by Mr Zech</a></iframe>

I Love You But We Must Fight is a solo journaling game where you play a single Hero tasked with defeating the Great Villain threatening the world. You’ve fought hard, sacrificed much, and now you are preparing for the final showdown. There’s only one hiccup: you love the Villain, and you wish to defeat them without destroying them. This game aims to tell stories like Star Wars and Naruto. To play, you need a copy of this game's rules, a deck of cards, a six-sided die. 

## Group Games

### Legacy Heroes
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/5337529" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/comic-book-death-legacy-heroes">Comic Book Death &amp; Legacy Heroes by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
Comic book characters are always dying, then coming back. On the big screen alone, we’ve seen Superman do so in Justice League and half the Marvel Cinematic Universe die and come back after the Blip. Some characters, like Jason Todd, are even defined by the manner of their death and return.

Legacy Heroes is a GM-less storytelling game for 1-5 players about the former sidekicks of a dead Hero who are doing their best to uphold their mentor's legacy and keep their City safe. This game is based on For the Queen, a product of Alex Roberts, and licensed for our use under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license. To play, you require a pack of standard playing cards and the booklet, or load the game online at the Storysynth link provided.

### Amber Court is NOT Haunted!
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/5213737" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/amber-court-is-not-haunted">Amber Court is NOT Haunted! by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
You are a senior citizen who is a member of Amber Court’s Home Owner Association (ACHOA). Your home’s value has plummeted due to the viral urban legend that the apartment building is haunted. Despite your threats of legal action, the conmen and influencers continue coming to Amber Court to film content and keep the legends alive. You’ve had enough! You’re taking matters into your own hands. Disrupt their filming and drive them off!

A one-page comedic one-shot for 2-5 players. This game was made for the Folklore Jam 2026, inspired by a Malaysian urban legend and this [actual press conference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EUBmSu4E0YY&t=3s&pp=ygUPYW1iZXIgY291cnQgbWNh) held by the home owners. A quick youtube search on Amber Court will bring up dozens of videos on the urban legend that the owners are eager to debunk -- and in the game the players will actively try to disrupt.

### To See the Sun
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/4964306" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/to-see-the-sun">To See The Sun by Mr Zech</a></iframe>

To See the Sun is a horror mystery game about a group of sorority girls caught in a time loop on the night of the biggest party of the year at their college. They are trying to uncover the identity of a masked murderer who is hunting them, and escape the time loop. This game is directly inspired by the movie Happy Death Day. 

The game is a one-shot Carved from Brindlewood game, meaning its mechanics and procedures are inspired by the games Brindlewood Bay and Public Access. 

### Bumbling Trio
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/4812838" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/bumbling-trio">Bumbling Trio by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
You are three characters who have difficulty with a lot of things that most of human society might take for granted. This could be because you are talking chipmunks who love to sing, three kobolds in a trench coat, or even just three weird humans. But with a little teamwork, you mostly get by. Play to find out whether the three of you will achieve your goals through the power of friendship.

Bumbling Trio is a GM-less comedic role-playing game with three player characters. Included are variant rules to play this as a duet (2-players) or with a traditional Game Master. This game uses four six-sided dice (d6).

### Die, Spawn of Santa
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/2997233" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/die-spawn-of-santa">Die, Spawn of Santa! by Mr Zech</a></iframe>

It’s the night before Christmas, and Santa Claus has left to deliver gifts to the children of the world. You and your fellow elves have just broken out the egg-nog, when the North Pole is attacked by black goat-like monsters. Can your ragtag group of elves hold back the swarm of monsters until Santa returns at dawn? Or will this be the North Pole’s last Christmas? 

### Pets & Fedoras
<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/2929719" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/pets-n-fedoras">Pets &amp; Fedoras by Mr Zech</a></iframe>
You are fedora-wearing special agents of the NNSA (NeverNamed Spy Agency) with cover identities as ordinary house pets belonging to a pair of genius children, Linneaus and Fern. Your mission, as always, is to find out what your evil scientist nemesis, Dr. Goofenheim, is up to and put a stop to it!

While you are away on mission, your genius owners are up to their own mad science mega-project. The story cuts back and forth between your Mission and the children’s project at Home.

This is an experimental diceless storytelling game for 1-5 players, which seeks to emulate Saturday morning weird science, spy cartoons like Phineas and Ferb.

### Hot Mutant Vigilantes

<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/2676441" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://mrzech.itch.io/hot-mutant-vigilantes">Hot Mutant Vigilantes by Mr Zech</a></iframe>

You are a HOT MUTANT VIGILANTE. More specifically, you are a student at a school for gifted youngsters where the extra-curricular activities involve dressing up in spandex and defending the world. The school has zero legal authority to do this, hence the label ‘vigilante’. The government and the humans hate you. This work is a hack of [Grant Howitt’s Sexy Battle Wizards](https://gshowitt.itch.io/sexy-battle-wizards) and is directly inspired by the X-men.
