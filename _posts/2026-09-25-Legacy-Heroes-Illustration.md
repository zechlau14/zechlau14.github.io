Legacy Heroes is a Descended from the Queen game, ie. it is based on [For the Queen](www.forthequeengame.com) by Alex Roberts.
In the original game, a set of Queen cards provide illustrations to inspire the players' imagination. So in Legacy Heroes, I have aspired to do the same.

When you define the Hero you lost together. You may use one of the Hero illustrations below as inspiration, or make up your own.
